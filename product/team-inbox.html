FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can multiple team members use the same WhatsApp number?

Yes. Multiple team members can share one number through the 11za shared inbox.

How do I make sure every chat has an owner?

Assign conversations to team members, or let them auto-assign to the right person. Each chat then has a clear owner.

Can my team reply from their phones?

Yes. The 11za app on Android and iPhone gives them the same shared inbox on the go.
