FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is the 11za API documentation public?

Yes. Anyone can read it without signing up, and the link is in the website footer.

Can I connect my CRM to 11za?

Most likely. CRMs and other tools usually connect through Pabbly or webhooks. Book a demo and our team will confirm the best way for your CRM.

Can my developers build their own integration?

Yes. They can use the public 11za API to send messages and read events from your own software.
