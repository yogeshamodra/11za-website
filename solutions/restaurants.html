FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can guests order food on WhatsApp?

Yes. Order and booking flows can be built in the chatbot. Book a demo to see an example for restaurants.

Can guests book a table in chat?

Yes. The bot takes booking requests and your team confirms them from the inbox. Reminders can go out before the booking.

Can the bot share my menu?

Yes. The bot can send the menu as images or a PDF, plus timings and location.

Can I send offers to past guests?

Yes. Broadcasts reach opted-in contacts with approved message templates.

Can I ask guests for feedback after a visit?

Yes. A thank-you and feedback message can go to guests after their visit. Replies land in your inbox.
