Our story

WhatsApp is where India does business. Most teams still run it from personal phones.

So we built the shared inbox, and then everything around it.

  1. 01

    The problem we kept seeing

    A customer messages a shop owner at night. The reply goes out the next morning, from a phone that only one person can see. When that person leaves, the chats leave with them.

  2. 02

    The idea: 1 Company – 1 Chat

    We started 11za to fix this for MSMEs. Your business gets one official WhatsApp number, and your whole team works from one shared inbox with roles, notes and full history.

  3. 03

    What we built around it

    Broadcasts to thousands of customers, no-code chatbots, Shopify and WooCommerce automations, and AI calls that reach new leads within seconds.

  4. 04

    Setup with a human, not a help page

    We handle Meta approval, connect your number, train your team and stay on call after you go live.

What we believe

Three ideas we build around.

One number, one team

Customers should talk to your business, not to one salesperson's phone. Every chat belongs to the company and stays with it.

Made for MSMEs

Plans are simple, setup takes days and the product fits how Indian teams actually sell: catalogues, COD, follow-ups and festival offers.

Official, with a human behind it

Everything runs on the official WhatsApp Business API, and a real person helps you from approval to blue tick.
