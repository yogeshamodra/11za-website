FAQ

Shared inbox, answered.

Is this the official WhatsApp Business API?

Yes. 11za is a Meta Business Partner, so your number runs on the official WhatsApp Business API.

Can my whole team use one number?

Yes. Add your team members with roles and permissions, and everyone replies from the same number.

What happens to chats when an agent leaves?

The conversations stay with the company. Reassign them to another agent and the full history is still there.

How long does setup take?

About 3 days. We handle Meta approval, connect your number, set up your team and train them before you go live.

Can I see who replied and how fast?

Yes. Reports show response times and workload for each agent, so you can see how the team is doing.
