FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is the WhatsApp Business API?

Meta's official WhatsApp for businesses. Unlike the free WhatsApp Business app, many team members can use one number, you can send broadcasts at scale, run chatbots and connect your other software.

Can I keep my current business number?

Yes, in most cases. The number moves to the API, so it can no longer be used in the regular WhatsApp app. Our team walks you through the switch.

What are WhatsApp conversation charges?

Meta charges per conversation, split into business-initiated and user-initiated conversations. These are separate from the 11za plan and billed as per Meta's rates.

How long does setup take?

Usually about 3 days: Meta approval, team setup and a 4-hour training session.

Is there a mobile app?

Yes. The 11za app is available on Android and iPhone, so your team can reply on the go.

What are AI Calls?

A new 11za feature: an AI voice agent that calls new leads within seconds, asks your qualifying questions, books visits or demos and logs a summary in your inbox.
